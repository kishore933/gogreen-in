A collection network for organic waste

Their waste is
somebody's raw material.

Stores and farms throw out spoiled produce, peels, husk, and stalks every day. Go Green logs it, matches it with composters, biogas plants, and small makers nearby, and tracks how much of it is diverted from landfill.

The process

Three steps, one working loop

  1. 01

    Source lists it

    A store or farm logs what they're throwing out — type, rough weight, and where to collect it.

  2. 02

    A maker claims it

    Composters, biogas operators, farmers, and small artisans browse open listings and mark one as collected.

  3. 03

    It becomes something

    The same waste turns into compost, feed, fiber, or fuel instead of rotting in a landfill.
